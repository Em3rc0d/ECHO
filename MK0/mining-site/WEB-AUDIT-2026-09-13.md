# Web Audit — 2026-09-13

## Método

Prioridad de fuentes:

1. estándares/especificaciones oficiales;
2. documentación oficial de proyectos/modelos;
3. repositorios originales;
4. papers/repositorios de autores;
5. datasets oficiales/DOI;
6. comparables operativos documentados.

No se certifica un claim solo por aparecer en un blog de terceros.

## Evidence nodes

### EV-AI-001 — YAMNet
Source: https://www.tensorflow.org/tutorials/audio/transfer_learning_audio  
Type: PRIMARY_DOC  
Claims certified: MobileNetV1; 521 AudioSet outputs; 16 kHz mono; 0.96 s frames; 0.48 s hop; 1024 embedding; transfer learning supported.

### EV-DATA-001 — AudioSet
Sources:
- https://research.google.com/audioset/
- https://research.google.com/audioset/download.html

Claims certified: 2,084,320 human-labeled 10-second segments; 527 annotated classes; metadata under CC BY 4.0; ontology CC BY-SA 4.0. YouTube media itself is not treated as redistributable ECHO asset by implication.

### EV-CLASS-001 — Alarm/Siren
Sources:
- https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/alarm_1.html
- https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/fire_alarm_1.html

Claims: AudioSet has Alarm/Siren/Fire alarm hierarchy; Siren and Fire alarm have meaningful public annotation counts.

### EV-CLASS-002 — Vehicle horn
Source: https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/vehicle_horn_car_horn_honking_2.html  
Claim: explicit acoustic event label with thousands of annotations.

### EV-CLASS-003 — Tire squeal
Source: https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/tire_squeal_1.html  
Claim: explicit vehicle tire friction acoustic label.

### EV-CLASS-004 — Shatter
Source: https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/shatter_1.html  
Claim: explicit brittle-object shatter acoustic label. ECHO uses `GLASS_SHATTER` only after dataset mapping/manual validation confirms glass semantics for selected assets.

### EV-DATA-002 — FSD50K
Source: https://zenodo.org/records/4060432  
Claims: 51,197 clips; 108.3 h; 200 classes; multilabel; uploader-disjoint dev/eval logic; per-clip mixed CC licenses. Asset-level license filter is mandatory.

### EV-DATA-003 — SONYC-UST
Source: https://zenodo.org/records/3966543  
Claims: real urban acoustic sensor network; multilabel; 23 fine / 8 coarse classes; sensor-disjoint training/validation and temporally displaced test; privacy-aware metadata quantization.

### EV-EVAL-001 — DCASE polyphonic SED
Sources:
- https://dcase.community/challenge2017/metrics
- https://dcase.community/challenge2024/task-sound-event-detection-with-heterogeneous-training-dataset-and-potentially-missing-labels

Claims: overlapping events require polyphonic-aware metrics; DCASE uses class-aware F1/pAUC/PSDS methodologies. Supports secondary PSDS evaluation when ECHO has strong temporal labels.

### EV-STREAM-001 — ONVIF Profile T
Sources:
- https://www.onvif.org/profiles/profile-t/
- https://www.onvif.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/ONVIF_Profile_T_Specification_v1-0.pdf

Claims: Profile T covers bidirectional audio; audio streaming can use RTSP; G.711/AAC requirements exist for conformant audio-capable implementations.

### EV-STREAM-002 — FFmpeg RTSP
Source: https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-protocols.html#rtsp  
Claims: RTSP demuxing and UDP/TCP transport supported. Certifies FFmpeg as baseline source adapter implementation, not universal camera compatibility.

### EV-STREAM-003 — GStreamer rtspsrc
Source: https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/documentation/rtsp/rtspsrc.html  
Claims: RTSP source, UDP/TCP negotiation, jitter removal/reordering and latency controls. Kept as fallback/challenger for streaming robustness.

### EV-BUS-001 — MQTT 5.0
Source: https://docs.oasis-open.org/mqtt/mqtt/v5.0/mqtt-v5.0.html  
Claim: QoS1 is at-least-once; duplicates may occur. Drives ECHO idempotency requirement.

### EV-BUS-002 — Mosquitto
Source: https://mosquitto.org/  
Claims: open-source MQTT broker; MQTT 5/3.1.1/3.1; lightweight; current site describes EPL/EDL licensing.

### EV-REF-001 — Frigate
Sources:
- https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/audio_detectors/
- https://docs.frigate.video/integrations/mqtt/

Claims: audio detector per camera, separate audio capture connection, RTSP-based camera streams, CPU acoustic detection and camera-scoped MQTT audio topics. Architectural precedent only.

### EV-PRIV-001 — Peru
Sources:

## Uncertainties deliberately preserved

- target-hardware benchmark;
- exact pretrained checkpoint redistribution rights where not explicit in source license;
- actual camera stream capabilities;
- domain-specific field performance;
- distance and SNR envelope;
- final model and thresholds.

These are empirical/external gates, not research gaps that should be guessed.
